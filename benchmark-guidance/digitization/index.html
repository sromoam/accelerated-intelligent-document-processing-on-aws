Digitization (OCR) Benchmarks

Section titled “Digitization (OCR) Benchmarks”

Digitization turns each page into page text that the later steps read. Digitization (OCR) explains how it works.

We don’t benchmark OCR on its own today. We measure its effect on the steps after it: each benchmark here scores the extraction accuracy an OCR reader produces, because that is what lands in your database. To compare readers on OCR alone, use public OCR benchmarks.

How to improve digitization on your documents

Section titled “How to improve digitization on your documents”

Your definitions. The reader doesn’t use them: it reads every page the same way. When a value is wrong, open View Page Text first. If the value is right in the page text, the fix is in your classification or extraction definitions.

Data quality. Scan at 300 DPI, and turn on preprocessing for photos and poor scans. See Improving digitization.

Settings.

SettingWhat it changesQuestions
OCR reader (ocr.backend)Amazon Textract, a vision model, or a specialized OCR modelDG-01 to DG-03
Resolution (ocr.image.dpi)The DPI the accelerator renders pages atDG-04
Preprocessing (ocr.image.preprocessing)Cleans up shadows, uneven lighting and low contrastDG-05

Questions

Section titled “Questions”
What the metrics mean
  • Weighted accuracy: Stickler’s field-weighted score against ground truth.
  • Documents with every field right: the share of documents with no wrong field.

Full definitions: Stickler’s Thresholds and Metrics, and Field Weighting in the evaluation guide.

Benchmark Datasets describes each dataset.

Does the OCR reader make a difference to extraction accuracy?

Section titled “Does the OCR reader make a difference to extraction accuracy?”

Bands show ±2 standard errors. Cost axes differ by dataset.

Answer: Amazon Textract with Layout only is a good place to start: on RealKIE invoices it scored as well as the other readers, for a third of the cost of Textract with tables. Test it on your own documents.

Details

Measured on release 0.6.15.dev1, November 2026, us-east-1. Source: 18 Test Studio runs (8 failed documents left out of the scores), listed in benchmarks/results/guidance/DG-01.json.

On OmniAI bank checks, Textract with Layout only got every field right on 47% of checks, against 16% with Amazon Nova 2 Lite, which cost less ($4.84 against $7.76 per 1,000).
